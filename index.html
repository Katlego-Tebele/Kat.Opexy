About Us

Floors That Make a Statement

Epoxy Flooring is a specialist flooring business dedicated to transforming ordinary surfaces into stunning, high-performance floors using premium decorative epoxy finishes.

We work with homeowners, business owners and property developers across South Africa to create floors that are as visually impressive as they are durable. From flowing marble effects and bold colour swirls to clean, minimalist gloss finishes — every floor we create is designed to become the defining feature of its space.

Epoxy flooring isn't just a coating — it's a transformation. Our decorative finishes use high-quality materials to produce seamless, glossy surfaces that resist wear, moisture and everyday damage while looking extraordinary for years to come.

The Benefits

Why Choose Epoxy Flooring?

Epoxy flooring combines beauty with serious performance — here's why more homeowners and businesses are choosing it.

Exceptionally Durable

Epoxy creates a tough, impact-resistant surface that stands up to heavy foot traffic, vehicles, dropped tools and daily wear without chipping or cracking.

Easy to Maintain

The seamless, non-porous surface means no grout lines to clean — just wipe or mop to keep your floor looking pristine. Resistant to stains, chemicals and moisture.

Glossy Premium Finish

Achieve a high-gloss, mirror-like surface that adds depth, light and luxury to any space — transforming ordinary floors into high-end design features.

Modern Appearance

Epoxy flooring suits contemporary design perfectly — sleek, seamless and available in a wide range of colours and patterns that complement any interior style.

Custom Designs

Choose from marble swirls, metallic finishes, solid colours or completely bespoke patterns. Your floor can be as unique as your space — no two designs are exactly alike.

Versatile Applications

Suitable for homes, garages, offices, showrooms, warehouses and retail spaces — epoxy flooring works across virtually any environment where performance and appearance both matter.
